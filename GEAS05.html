MODULE 1

RA 9292: The Electronics Engineering Law of 2004 & Ethics

Date Approved: April 17, 2004 | SB 2683 & HB 5224

Key Statutory Definitions (RA 9292 Section 3) [NOTES & HANDWRITTEN ANNOTATIONS]

Electronics

Science and technology dealing with the flow and control of electrons/carriers in vacuum, gases, conductors, semiconductors, and non-linear dielectrics.

Telecommunications

Any transmission, emission, or reception of signs, signals, writings, images, sounds, or intelligence of any nature by wire, radio, optical or other systems.

Broadcasting

Transmission of audio and/or video programs by radio, cable, or other means intended for direct reception by the general public.

ICT (Info & Comms Tech)

Acquisition, production, processing, storage, and dissemination of vocal, pictorial, textual, and numerical information by microelectronics and telecom.

Computer System

Any device or collection of devices that performs data processing functions according to pre-programmed instructions.

APO (Accredited Prof. Org.)

The officially recognized integrated professional organization for electronics engineering in the Philippines: IECEP.

Articles I & II: Administration Sec 1–12

  • • Art. I: General Provisions (Sec 1-5): Policy, scope, definitions of ECT, ECE, PECE.
  • • Art. II: PRB-BECE (Sec 6-12): Board creation, powers, and governance.
  • • Composition: 1 Chairman + 2 Members (Total = 3) appointed by the President of the Philippines.
  • • Board Qualifications: Filipino citizen, at least 31 years old, active PECE, 10+ years experience (teaching allowed), 5+ years active IECEP member, no moral turpitude conviction.
  • • Term: 3 years per term; max 2 terms (6 years maximum tenure).
  • • Grounds for Removal: Continuous neglect of duty, incompetence, malpractice, or unprofessional conduct.
  • • Custodian of Records: Professional Regulation Commission (PRC).

Article III: Exam & Licensure Sec 13–26

  • • ECE Exam Scope: Mathematics, GEAS, EST (Electronics Systems & Tech), Electronics.
  • • Passing Grade: General Weighted Average $\ge \mathbf{70\%}$, with no subject rating below $\mathbf{50\%}$.
  • • Release of Results: Released within 3 days (72 hours).
  • • CSC Equivalency: PRC license automatically confers Professional Eligibility.
  • • Revocation / Reinstatement: Revoked license can be reinstated after 2 years upon formal petition.
  • • APO Dues Delinquency: Non-payment of dues results in being listed as delinquent in the official roster.
  • • Foreign Special Permit: Granted for specialized projects, valid for 6 months.

Articles IV–VIII: Practice & Penal Sec 27–43

  • • Art. IV Practice (Sec 27-30): Practice is strictly individual. NO corporate practice permitted under Sec 28!
  • • Art. V Sundry Provisions (Sec 31-34): Foreign reciprocity, positions in government, rosters.
  • • Art. VI Penal Provisions (Sec 35-37): Fines of ₱100,000 to ₱1,000,000, imprisonment from 6 months to 6 years, or both.
  • • Seal Lending Violation: Borrowing or lending a PECE seal results in criminal liability for BOTH parties.
  • • Art. VII Transitory Provisions (Sec 38-40): Existing licenses grandfathered under old RA 5734.
  • • Art. VIII Final Provisions (Sec 41-43): Appropriations, repealing clause, effectivity.

1. Coulomb's Law of Electrostatic Force

🔴 MUST MEMORIZE
$$F_e = k_e \frac{|q_1 q_2|}{r^2}$$

• Variables: $F_e\ [\text{N}]$, $q_1, q_2\ [\text{C}]$, $r\ [\text{m}]$ distance.

• Coulomb Constant: $k_e = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \approx 9.0 \times 10^9\ \text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}^2$.

• Scaling Trap: If distance is halved ($r \to r/2$), Force quadruples ($F_e \to 4F_e$).

• 3-Charge Equilibrium: For two charges $+Q$ at distance $d$, central charge must be: $$q = -\frac{Q}{4}$$

Point Charge E-Field: $E = \frac{k_e q}{r^2}$ Board Exam Problem
Result: 72,000 N/C (7.2 × 10⁴ N/C)

2. Lorentz Magnetic Force & Trajectory

🔴 MUST MEMORIZE
$$F_B = q v B \sin\theta$$

• Proton Problem: $v = 3.0 \times 10^5\ \text{m/s}$, $B = 0.4\ \text{T}$, $\theta = 90^\circ$: $$F_B = (1.602 \times 10^{-19})(3.0 \times 10^5)(0.4) = \mathbf{1.92 \times 10^{-14}\ \text{N}}$$

• Particle Trajectories:
  • $\theta = 0^\circ \implies F_B = 0 \implies$ Straight Line
  • $\theta = 90^\circ \implies$ Circular Orbit ($r = \frac{mv}{qB}$)
  • $0^\circ < \theta < 90^\circ \implies$ Helical (Spiral)

• Orbit Scaling: If velocity $v$ is doubled, orbit radius $r$ is doubled.

• Right Hand Rule: Velocity $\vec{v}$ UP ($\uparrow$), $\vec{B}$ into page ($\otimes$) $\implies$ Force is TO THE LEFT.

3. Current-Carrying Wire & Motional EMF

🟠 HIGH-YIELD
$$F = I L B \sin\theta$$
$$\mathcal{E} = B L v \sin\theta$$

• Conductor Force Problem: $L = 2.0\ \text{m}$, $I = 5.0\ \text{A}$, $B = 0.1\ \text{T}$, $\theta = 90^\circ$: $$F = (5.0)(2.0)(0.1) = \mathbf{1.0\ \text{N}}$$

• Motional EMF Problem: $L = 0.5\ \text{m}$, $v = 10\ \text{m/s}$, $B = 0.2\ \text{T}$: $$\mathcal{E} = (0.2)(0.5)(10) = \mathbf{1.0\ \text{V}}$$

• Electric Work & Voltage: $W = q \Delta V \implies \Delta V = \frac{20\ \text{J}}{4\ \text{C}} = \mathbf{5.0\ \text{V}}$.

• Uniform E-Field Acceleration: Electron released from rest near negative plate moves toward positive plate with constant acceleration ($a = eE/m$).

4. Faraday's Law & Lenz's Law

🟠 HIGH-YIELD
$$\mathcal{E} = -N \frac{d\Phi_B}{dt} = -N \frac{d(B A \cos\theta)}{dt}$$

• Lenz's Law Principle: Opposes the change in magnetic flux; manifestation of Conservation of Energy.

• Resistance Trap: Using wire of higher resistance does NOT decrease induced EMF! (It reduces current $I = \mathcal{E}/R$, but $\mathcal{E}$ is unchanged).

• Rotating Generator Coil: Induced EMF is MAXIMUM when coil plane is PARALLEL to B-field (normal vector at $90^\circ$, flux is zero, rate $\frac{d\Phi}{dt}$ is peak!).

• Adjacent Loops A & B: If current in Loop A increases suddenly, Loop B experiences a Repulsive Force.

AC Circuits & DC Transients Formula Suite

Vector voltages, phase angles, and rise time calculators

Series RLC Phase Angle

[SOLVED]
$$\tan\theta = \frac{X_L - X_C}{R}$$

Board Exam Problem:
$R = 10\ \Omega$, $X_L = 40\ \Omega$, $X_C = 30\ \Omega$ $$\tan\theta = \frac{40 - 30}{10} = \frac{10}{10} = 1$$ $$\theta = \arctan(1) = \mathbf{+45^\circ}$$

Circuit is inductive; voltage leads current by $+45^\circ$.

Series RC Vector Triangle

⚠️ VECTOR TRAP
$$V_S = \sqrt{V_R^2 + V_C^2} \implies V_C = \sqrt{V_S^2 - V_R^2}$$

Exam Trap: NEVER do scalar subtraction ($120 - 72 = 48\ \text{V}$ is wrong!).

$V_S = 120\ \text{V}_{\text{rms}}$, $V_R = 72\ \text{V}_{\text{rms}}$ $$V_C = \sqrt{120^2 - 72^2} = \sqrt{9216} = \mathbf{96\ \text{V}_{\text{rms}}}$$

• High Frequency Behavior: $$X_C = \frac{1}{2\pi f C} \to 0 \quad\text{as}\quad f \to \infty$$ Capacitor acts as an AC Short Circuit.

DC RL Rise Time to 50%

🟠 HIGH-YIELD
$$i(t) = I_{\max}(1 - e^{-t/\tau}) \implies t_{50\%} = \tau \ln(2)$$

Board Exam Problem:
$L = 300\ \text{mH} = 0.3\ \text{H}$, $R = 2\ \Omega$, $V = 2\ \text{V}$
Time constant: $\tau = \frac{L}{R} = \frac{0.3}{2} = 0.15\ \text{s}$ $$t = 0.15 \times \ln(2) = 0.15 \times 0.69315$$ t = 0.104 seconds

Don't Confuse These! (Board Exam Pitfall Matrix)

High error-rate multiple-choice pairs

1. ECE vs. PECE Scope & Seal Authority RA 9292 Sec 27

ECE (Registered)

• Passed Board Licensure Exam.

• Designs, supervises, operates.

• CANNOT seal or sign permit plans!

PECE (Professional)

• Valid ECE + 7 years active practice.

• 2 PECE endorsements + interview.

• SOLE legal authority to seal & sign plans!

2. Nuisance Alarm vs. False Alarm PEC Vol 2 / RA 9514

Nuisance Alarm

• Triggered by equipment defect, dirt, dust, improper installation, or poor maintenance.

• Resolved by engineering servicing.

False Alarm (Malicious)

• Triggered by human mischief, malicious pull, or prank without any fire hazard.

• Governed by penal provisions.

3. Generator Coil: Parallel vs. Perpendicular to B-Field Electromagnetics

Plane PARALLEL to Field

• Normal vector at $\theta = 90^\circ$.

• Flux through coil $\Phi_B = 0$.

• Induced EMF is MAXIMUM ($\frac{d\Phi}{dt}$ peak)!

Plane PERPENDICULAR to Field

• Normal vector at $\theta = 0^\circ$.

• Flux through coil is MAXIMUM.

• Induced EMF is ZERO ($\frac{d\Phi}{dt} = 0$)!

4. Capacitor vs. Inductor at High Frequency AC Circuit Asymptotes

Capacitor at High $f$

• $X_C = \frac{1}{2\pi f C} \to 0\ \Omega$.

• Acts as an AC SHORT CIRCUIT.

• Blocks DC, passes high freq.

Inductor at High $f$

• $X_L = 2\pi f L \to \infty\ \Omega$.

• Acts as an AC OPEN CIRCUIT.

• Passes DC, chokes high freq.
