🚗 KINEMATICS & DYNAMICS

🔴 ESSENTIAL

Overtaking Trains: $t = \frac{L_1 + L_2}{v_1 - v_2}$ (same direction)

Free-Fall Symmetry: Time to peak $t_{peak} = \frac{t_{up} + t_{down}}{2}$

Vertical Wall Friction: Min force to hold block: $P_{min} = \frac{mg}{\mu_s}$

Apparent Weight (Elevator): $N = m(g \pm a)$

TRAP: On vertical wall, $N = P$, so $f_s = \mu P = mg$. Do NOT use $N = mg$!

💥 WORK, ENERGY & IMPULSE

🟠 HIGH-YIELD

Constant Power Velocity: $v \propto t^{1/2}$ ($P = Fv = ma\cdot v$)

Hanging Chain Work: $W = \frac{MgL}{18}$ for $1/3$ length hanging

Momentum vs KE: $KE = \frac{p^2}{2m} \implies +20\% p \rightarrow +44\% KE$

Impulse Comparison: Bouncing ball delivers $2\times$ impulse vs sticking clay ball!

Crumple Zone: $\uparrow \Delta t \implies \downarrow F_{avg}$ since $J = F_{avg}\Delta t = \Delta p$.

🔄 ROTATIONAL DYNAMICS

🔴 MUST MEMORIZE

Incline Race: Solid Cylinder ($I=\frac{1}{2}mr^2$) BEATS Hollow Sphere ($I=\frac{2}{3}mr^2$)

Flywheel Expansion: Heated flywheel $\rightarrow R\uparrow \rightarrow I\uparrow \rightarrow \omega\downarrow$ (Cons. of $L$)

Rolling vs Sliding: $KE_{roll} = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}I\omega^2 > KE_{slide}$ at same $v$

Newton 2nd Law (Rotation): $\tau_{net} = I\alpha$

Skater pulling arms in: $I\downarrow \implies \omega\uparrow \implies KE_{rot}\uparrow$ (work done by muscles)!

🌊 SHM & WAVES

🟠 HIGH-YIELD

Parallel Springs: $k_{eq} = 2k \implies \omega = \sqrt{\frac{2k}{m}}$

SHM Energy Ratio: When $KE = 3 PE \implies x = \pm \frac{A}{2}$

Wave Speed: $y(x,t) = A\sin(kx - \omega t) \implies v = \frac{\omega}{k}$

String Wave Speed: $v = \sqrt{\frac{T}{\mu}}$ ($\uparrow T \implies \uparrow v$; $\uparrow \mu \implies \downarrow v$)

Sound wave = Longitudinal pressure wave caused by molecular oscillation.

📢 ACOUSTICS & SOUND

🟡 IMPORTANT

Inverse Square Law: $I = \frac{P}{4\pi r^2} \implies 3\times \text{distance} = \frac{1}{9} \text{intensity}$

Doppler Moving Source AWAY: $f_{observed} = f_{source} \left(\frac{v}{v + v_s}\right)$

Doppler Moving Source TOWARD: $f_{observed} = f_{source} \left(\frac{v}{v - v_s}\right)$

TRAP: Approaching source $\rightarrow f_o > f_s$ (+ pitch); Receding source $\rightarrow f_o < f_s$ (- pitch).

🔍 OPTICS & LENSES

🔴 MUST MEMORIZE

TIR Conditions: 1. Dense $\to$ Rare ($n_1 > n_2$) & 2. $\theta_i > \theta_c$

Convex Mirror: ALWAYS Virtual, Upright, Diminished ($f = -R/2$)

Lensmaker's Equation: $\frac{1}{f} = (n-1)\left(\frac{1}{R_1} - \frac{1}{R_2}\right)$

Biconvex Lens Sign: $R_1 > 0$, $R_2 < 0$ (Cartesian sign convention)

Glass biconvex ($n=1.5, R_1=10, R_2=-15\text{ cm}$) $\implies f = +12\text{ cm}$.

KINEMATICS 🔴 MUST MEMORIZE

PROJECTILE MAXIMUM HEIGHT EQUALS RANGE ($H = R$)

$$\tan\theta = 4 \implies \theta = \arctan(4) \approx 75.96^\circ$$

DERIVATION: $H = \frac{v_0^2 \sin^2\theta}{2g}$, $R = \frac{v_0^2 \sin(2\theta)}{g} = \frac{2 v_0^2 \sin\theta \cos\theta}{g}$. Set $H=R \implies \frac{\sin\theta}{2} = 2\cos\theta \implies \tan\theta = 4$.

USE WHEN: Asked for launch angle where apex height equals horizontal distance over flat ground.

⚠️ COMMON TRAP: Do NOT confuse with maximum range angle which is $\theta = 45^\circ$!

ENERGY & DYNAMICS 🟠 HIGH-YIELD

WORK TO PULL HANGING CHAIN ONTO TABLE

$$W = \Delta PE = m_{hang} g h_{cm} = \left(\frac{M}{3}\right) g \left(\frac{L}{6}\right) = \frac{M g L}{18}$$

VARIABLES: $M$ = Total Mass [kg], $L$ = Total Length [m], $g = 9.81\text{ m/s}^2$.

USE WHEN: $1/3$ of uniform chain hangs over frictionless edge.

⚠️ COMMON TRAP: Center of mass of hanging portion is at $L/6$, NOT $L/3$!

ROTATION 🔴 MUST MEMORIZE

MOTOR TORQUE & ANGULAR ACCELERATION

$$\tau = I \alpha, \quad I_{disk} = \frac{1}{2} m R^2, \quad \alpha = \frac{\omega}{t} = \frac{2\pi f}{t}$$

VARIABLES: $\tau$ [N·m], $I$ [kg·m²], $\alpha$ [rad/s²], $f$ [rev/s or rpm/60].

EXAMPLE: $50\text{ kg}$ disk, $R=0.4\text{ m}$, $1200\text{ rpm}$ in $8\text{ s} \implies I=4\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m}^2, \alpha=5\pi\text{ rad/s}^2, \tau=20\pi \approx 62.83\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$.

⚠️ COMMON TRAP: Convert rpm to rad/s first ($\omega = \frac{\text{rpm} \cdot 2\pi}{60}$).

OPTICS 🔴 MUST MEMORIZE

LENSMAKER'S EQUATION (THIN LENS)

$$\frac{1}{f} = (n - 1) \left( \frac{1}{R_1} - \frac{1}{R_2} \right)$$

SIGN CONVENTION: Convex surface facing light $\implies R > 0$. Concave surface facing light $\implies R < 0$.

EXAMPLE: Biconvex $n=1.5, R_1 = +10\text{ cm}, R_2 = -15\text{ cm} \implies \frac{1}{f} = 0.5 \left(\frac{1}{10} + \frac{1}{15}\right) = \frac{1}{12} \implies f = +12\text{ cm}$.

⚠️ COMMON TRAP: $R_2$ for a biconvex lens is NEGATIVE in standard Cartesian convention!

ACOUSTICS 🟠 HIGH-YIELD

DOPPLER EFFECT (MOVING SOURCE)

$$f_o = f_s \left( \frac{v}{v \pm v_s} \right) \quad \text{(+ when moving AWAY, - when TOWARD)}$$

EXAMPLE: $1000\text{ Hz}$ siren moving away at $34\text{ m/s}$, $v=340\text{ m/s} \implies f_o = 1000 \left(\frac{340}{340 + 34}\right) = 909.09\text{ Hz}$.

⚠️ COMMON TRAP: Source moving AWAY increases denominator, lowering observed frequency!

SHM 🟠 HIGH-YIELD

PARALLEL SPRINGS FREQUENCY & SHM ENERGY

$$k_{eq} = k_1 + k_2 = 2k \implies \omega = \sqrt{\frac{2k}{m}}; \quad \text{When } KE = 3PE \implies x = \pm \frac{A}{2}$$

DERIVATION: Total $E = KE + PE = 4PE \implies \frac{1}{2}kA^2 = 4(\frac{1}{2}kx^2) \implies x^2 = A^2/4 \implies x = \pm A/2$.

⚠️ Side-by-Side Critical Comparisons

1. ROLLING VS SLIDING (WHEELS AT SAME SPEED)

Wheel A: Rolling without slipping

Has BOTH translational and rotational kinetic energy.

$KE_{total} = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}I\omega^2$

⚡ MORE Kinetic Energy!

Wheel B: Sliding on ice

Has ONLY translational kinetic energy (no rotation).

$KE_{total} = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$

LESS Kinetic Energy

2. HOLLOW SPHERE VS SOLID CYLINDER ON INCLINE

Solid Cylinder ($I = \frac{1}{2}mr^2$)

Smaller shape factor $c = 0.5$. Less rotational inertia resist.

$a = \frac{g\sin\theta}{1 + 1/2} = \frac{2}{3}g\sin\theta$

🏆 WINS RACE (Reaches bottom first)

Hollow Sphere ($I = \frac{2}{3}mr^2$)

Larger shape factor $c = 0.67$. Mass concentrated at edge.

$a = \frac{g\sin\theta}{1 + 2/3} = \frac{3}{5}g\sin\theta$

Slower acceleration

3. BOUNCING RUBBER BALL VS STICKING CLAY BALL

Rubber Ball (Bounces back)

Velocity changes from $+v$ to $-v$.

$\Delta p = m(-v) - mv = -2mv$

⚡ Exerts $2\times$ GREATER Impulse on wall!

Clay Ball (Sticks to wall)

Velocity changes from $+v$ to $0$.

$\Delta p = 0 - mv = -mv$

Exerts half the impulse

4. HORIZONTAL INCLINE SLIDING VS CONSTANT VELOCITY

Impending Motion (Static)

Angle where block JUST begins to slide.

$\mu_s = \tan\theta_{repose}$

Constant Velocity Motion (Kinetic)

Sliding down at constant speed ($a=0$).

$\mu_k = \tan\theta$

📊 CRITICAL PHYSICAL CONSTANTS & UNIT CONVERSIONS

GRAVITY ($g$) $9.81\text{ m/s}^2 = 32.2\text{ ft/s}^2$
SPEED OF SOUND (AIR) $v_{sound} = 340\text{ m/s}$
AIR INDEX OF REFRACTION $n_{air} = 1.00$
GLASS REFRACTIVE INDEX $n_{glass} \approx 1.50$

🌳 DECISION TREE: WHICH INCLINE / FRICTION EQUATION TO USE?

1. Is block pressed against vertical wall?
└─ YES $\to N = P \implies f_s = \mu P = mg \implies P_{min} = \frac{mg}{\mu}$
2. Is block on inclined plane $\theta$?
├─ Normal force $N = mg\cos\theta$
├─ Downslope force $F_\parallel = mg\sin\theta$
└─ Just sliding on own weight $\to \mu = \tan\theta$
3. Is block sliding at CONSTANT VELOCITY down incline?
└─ YES $\to F_{net} = 0 \implies \mu_k = \tan\theta$

🌳 DECISION TREE: ROTATIONAL MOTION PROBLEMS

1. Is external torque applied? ($\tau_{ext} \neq 0$)
└─ YES $\to$ Use $\tau = I\alpha$, where $\alpha = \Delta\omega / t$
2. No external torque? ($\tau_{ext} = 0$)
└─ YES $\to$ Conserve Angular Momentum: $I_1\omega_1 = I_2\omega_2$
3. Is object rolling down incline without slipping?
└─ YES $\to a = \frac{g\sin\theta}{1 + c}$ ($c=1/2$ disk, $c=2/3$ hollow sphere)
