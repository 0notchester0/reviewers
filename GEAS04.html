CORE FINANCIAL MATHEMATICS FORMULAS

1. Compound Interest
$$F = P(1+i)^n = P\left(1 + \frac{NR}{m}\right)^{m \cdot t}$$

USE WHEN: Single future lump sum $F$ or present $P$.

TRAP: $i = NR/m$ per compounding period!

2. Real Interest Rate (Exact vs Approx)
$$i_{\text{real}} = \frac{1 + i_{\text{nom}}}{1 + f} - 1 \approx i_{\text{nom}} - f$$

USE WHEN: Accounting for inflation rate $f$ (Q2).

TRAP: Board exam usually defaults to exact form!

3. Amortization (Present Worth)
$$P = A \left[ \frac{(1+i)^n - 1}{i(1+i)^n} \right]$$

USE WHEN: Finding loan monthly payment $A$ (Q3).

TRAP: Subtract downpayment first from price!

4. Sinking Fund (Future Worth)
$$F = A \left[ \frac{(1+i)^n - 1}{i} \right]$$

USE WHEN: Savings goal targeted in future (Q4).

TRAP: Multiply individual target by total headcount!

STARTUP METRICS & FINANCIAL EQUATIONS

Net Burn Rate & Runway
$$\text{Net Burn} = \text{OpEx} - \text{Revenue}$$ $$\text{Runway (mo)} = \frac{\text{Cash Balance}}{\text{Net Burn Rate}}$$
Gross Margin Percentage
$$\text{COGS} = \text{Direct Materials} + \text{Direct Labor}$$ $$\text{GM\%} = \frac{\text{Price} - \text{COGS}}{\text{Price}} \times 100\%$$
Valuation & Ownership
$$\text{Post-Money} = \text{Pre-Money} + \text{Investment}$$ $$\text{Investor \%} = \frac{\text{Investment}}{\text{Post-Money}}$$
Accounting Fundamental Equation
$$\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Equity}$$
• Loan for inventory: Assets +100k, Liab +100k

Method Annual Charge Formula ($d$) Key Characteristic / Rule Solved Value (Q8: $FC=380\text{k}, SV=19.5\text{k}, n=10$)

Sunk Cost vs. Opportunity Cost
Sunk Cost (Q11) • Cost incurred in the past
• Unrecoverable expense
• ACTION: MUST BE IGNORED in future economic decisions!
Opportunity Cost (Q12) • Potential benefit given up
• Foregone value of next option
• ACTION: INCLUDE when evaluating trade-offs!
Cost-Plus vs. Value-Based Pricing
Cost-Plus Pricing • Total Cost + Fixed Mark-up %
• Based strictly on production cost
• Traditional pricing method
Value-Based Pricing (Q52) • Based on estimated financial gain
• Based on customer perceived value
• Common for SaaS optimization

TOP 5 BOARD EXAM TRAPS TO AVOID

1. COGS vs Overhead

In Gross Margin %, include ONLY direct materials & labor. IGNORE office rent/admin overhead (Q26)!

2. Salvage Value in DDBM

DDBM rate $k = 2/n$ IGNORES salvage value $SV$ when calculating rate percentage!

3. Pre vs Post-Money %

Investor ownership % = Investment / Post-Money. Post-Money = Pre-Money + Investment (Q29)!

4. Unfair Advantage Entry

"We have the best developers" is a WEAK Lean Canvas entry because it is easily claimed/copied (Q20)!

5. Inflation Interest Rate

Real interest rate $i_{\text{real}} = \frac{1+i_{\text{nom}}}{1+f} - 1$. Don't confuse with simple subtraction!
