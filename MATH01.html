CORE CHEAT SHEET — TOP 12 BOARD EXAM FORMULAS

1. Fundamental Derivative 🔴 MUST MEMORIZE
$$\lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h} = f'(x)$$

USE: Evaluating theoretical limits representing exact instantaneous derivatives.

[NOTES] Used in Question 5 to instantly spot $\frac{d}{dx}(\sin x)$ at $x = \pi/6$.
2. Composite Chain Rule 🔴 MUST MEMORIZE
$$\frac{d}{dx}[f(g(u))] = f'(g(u)) \cdot g'(u) \cdot u'$$

USE: Multi-layered composite function differentiation.

[NOTES] Question 7: $h'(x) = f'(g(x^2)) \cdot g'(x^2) \cdot 2x \implies h'(2) = -60$.
3. Position-Dependent Acceleration 🔴 MUST MEMORIZE
$$a(x) = v \frac{dv}{dx}$$

USE: When velocity is given as a function of position $v(x)$ instead of time $t$.

⚠️ TRAP: Do not just differentiate $v(x)$! You MUST multiply by $v(x)$.
4. Inverse Function Derivative 🔴 MUST MEMORIZE
$$(f^{-1})'(b) = \frac{1}{f'(a)} \quad \text{where } f(a) = b$$

USE: Evaluating slope of inverse functions without solving for $f^{-1}(x)$.

⚠️ TRAP: Do NOT compute $\frac{1}{f'(b)}$! First solve $f(a)=b$ to get $a$.
5. Cartesian Radius of Curvature 🔴 MUST MEMORIZE
$$\rho = \frac{[1 + (y')^2]^{3/2}}{|y''|}$$

USE: Curves $y = f(x)$. Note that $\rho = 1/\kappa$ (reciprocal of curvature).

[NOTES] Question 16: $y=\ln x$ at $x=1 \implies y'=1, y''=-1 \implies \rho = 2\sqrt{2}$.
6. Parametric Radius of Curvature 🟠 HIGH-YIELD
$$\rho = \frac{[(x')^2 + (y')^2]^{3/2}}{|x' y'' - y' x''|}$$

USE: Parametrically defined curves $x(t), y(t)$.

[NOTES] Curvature $\kappa$ is the inverse: $\kappa = \frac{|x' y'' - y' x''|}{[(x')^2 + (y')^2]^{3/2}}$.
7. Linear Approximation 🟠 HIGH-YIELD
$$f(x_0 + \Delta x) \approx f(x_0) + f'(x_0)\Delta x$$

USE: Estimating nearby function values or differential error propagation.

[NOTES] Question 19: $f(2)=5, f'(2)=3 \implies f(2.04) \approx 5 + 3(0.04) = 5.12$.
8. Euler's Theorem (Homogeneous) 🔴 MUST MEMORIZE
$$x \frac{\partial V}{\partial x} + y \frac{\partial V}{\partial y} = n \cdot V(x,y)$$

USE: Shortcuts multivariable partial differential expressions for homogeneous degree $n$.

[NOTES] Question 34: $V=\frac{x^4-y^4}{x^2+y^2}$ has degree $n=2 \implies 2V(x,y)$.
9. Hessian Discriminant ($D$) 🔴 MUST MEMORIZE
$$D = f_{xx} f_{yy} - (f_{xy})^2$$

CRITERIA: $D > 0, f_{xx} > 0$ (Min) | $D > 0, f_{xx} < 0$ (Max) | $D < 0$ (Saddle Point).

[NOTES] Question 36/37: $D < 0$ strictly guarantees a Saddle Point!
10. Newton-Raphson Iteration 🔴 MUST MEMORIZE
$$x_{n+1} = x_n - \frac{f(x_n)}{f'(x_n)}$$

USE: Rapid numerical root iteration.

⚠️ CRITICAL FAIL: Geometrically breaks down if $f'(x_n) = 0$ (division by zero).
11. Bisection Min. Iterations 🔴 MUST MEMORIZE
$$N \ge \frac{\ln\left(\frac{b - a}{\epsilon}\right)}{\ln 2}$$

USE: Calculating exact iterations needed for error tolerance $\epsilon$ on $[a,b]$.

[NOTES] Question 27: $[0,1], \epsilon=0.001 \implies N \ge 9.96 \implies 10 \text{ iterations}$.
12. Central Difference Error 🟠 HIGH-YIELD
$$\text{Truncation Error} \propto h^2$$

USE: Error scaling in numerical differentiation.

[NOTES] Question 22: Halving step $h \to h/2$ reduces error by a factor of $1/4$.

🔴 INDETERMINATE FORMS & L'HÔPITAL'S RULE

🔴 MUST MEMORIZE
Standard Quotient Forms ($\frac{0}{0}$ or $\frac{\infty}{\infty}$):
$$\lim_{x \to a} \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} = \lim_{x \to a} \frac{f'(x)}{g'(x)}$$

Differentiate numerator and denominator separately until an indeterminate state is removed.

Algebraic Transformation Strategies:
  • • Product $0 \cdot \infty$: Rewrite as $\frac{f(x)}{1/g(x)}$ to form $\frac{0}{0}$ or $\frac{\infty}{\infty}$.
  • • Difference $\infty - \infty$: Combine into a single common fraction first.
  • • Powers $1^\infty, 0^0, \infty^0$: Take natural log $y = f(x)^{g(x)} \implies \ln y = g(x) \ln f(x)$.

🟠 MULTIVARIABLE PATH DEPENDENCE Question 4 [NOTES]
$$\lim_{(x,y) \to (0,0)} \frac{xy}{x^2 + y^2}$$

Test along linear path $y = mx$:

$$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x(mx)}{x^2 + (mx)^2} = \frac{m x^2}{x^2(1 + m^2)} = \frac{m}{1 + m^2}$$

Result depends directly on slope $m \implies$ LIMIT DOES NOT EXIST (DNE)!

$f^{(100)}(\pi) = \cos(\pi) = -1$

Question 18 [NOTES]

$$dy = f'(x) dx$$

2. Relative Error
$$\text{Relative Error} = \frac{dy}{y}$$
Ratio of absolute error to actual function value.
3. Percentage Error
$$\text{Percentage} = \frac{dy}{y} \times 100\%$$
For sphere volume $V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 \implies \frac{dV}{V} = 3 \frac{dr}{r}$.
Sphere Volume Error Example (Q18): $r = 10\text{ m}, dr = \pm 0.1\text{ m} \implies dV = 4\pi r^2 dr = 4\pi(10)^2(\pm 0.1) = \mathbf{\pm 40\pi\ m^3}$.

PAGE 4: CURVATURE, GEOMETRY & MULTIVARIABLE CALCULUS

Curvature, center of curvature, Clairaut's, Euler's, Hessian discriminant, gradients, and Jacobians

CENTER OF CURVATURE $(\alpha, \beta)$ & OSCULATING CIRCLE

X-Coordinate of Center ($\alpha$):
$$\alpha = x - \frac{y'[1 + (y')^2]}{y''}$$
Y-Coordinate of Center ($\beta$):
$$\beta = y + \frac{1 + (y')^2}{y''}$$

The Osculating Circle has center $(\alpha, \beta)$ and radius equal to the radius of curvature $\rho = 1/\kappa$. Equation: $(x - \alpha)^2 + (y - \beta)^2 = \rho^2$.

TOTAL DIFFERENTIAL ($dz$)
$$dz = \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} dx + \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} dy$$

Used to estimate physical changes in multivariable ECE systems (e.g., total voltage drift $dV = V_I dI + V_R dR$).

GRADIENT VECTOR ($\nabla f$)
$$\nabla f = \left\langle \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}, \frac{\partial f}{\partial z} \right\rangle$$

Points in the direction of maximum rate of increase. $|\nabla f|$ equals the maximum rate of change.

🔴 SECOND PARAMETRIC DERIVATIVE FORMULA Question 15 [NOTES]
$$\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = \frac{\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)}{\frac{dx}{dt}}$$
⚠️ CRITICAL TRAP: $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} \ne \frac{y''(t)}{x''(t)}$! You MUST divide the $t$-derivative of $\frac{dy}{dx}$ by $x'(t)$. For Question 15 ($x=t^2, y=t^3-3t$), evaluation at $t=1$ gives 1.5.
🔵 CLAIRAUT'S (SCHWARZ'S) THEOREM Question 35 [NOTES]
$$f_{xy} = f_{yx} \quad \left(\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x \partial y} = \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y \partial x}\right)$$

Sufficient Condition: Requires CONTINUOUS SECOND-ORDER partial derivatives.

🔴 JACOBIAN DETERMINANT Question 38 [NOTES]
$$J = \begin{vmatrix} \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} \\ \frac{\partial v}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial v}{\partial y} \end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ 1 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = 2(3) - (-1)(1) = 7$$

For $u=2x-y$ and $v=x+3y$, exact absolute value of Jacobian is 7.

PAGE 5: NUMERICAL METHODS & ALGORITHMS

Finite differences, root-finding algorithms, numerical integration error bounds

FINITE DIFFERENCE DERIVATIVE APPROXIMATIONS

Forward Difference $O(h)$
$$f'(x) \approx \frac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h}$$
First-order accurate using current and forward point $x+h$.
Backward Difference $O(h)$
$$f'(x) \approx \frac{f(x) - f(x-h)}{h}$$
First-order accurate using current and backward point $x-h$.
Central Difference $O(h^2)$
$$f'(x) \approx \frac{f(x+h) - f(x-h)}{2h}$$
Second-order accurate! Halving step $h \to h/2$ reduces error by $1/4$.
Second-Order Difference Operator ($\Delta^2 V$):
$$\Delta^2 V(x) = V(x+2h) - 2V(x+h) + V(x)$$
Question 21 Example: $V(0)=10, V(2)=18, V(4)=30 \implies \Delta^2 V(0) = (30-18) - (18-10) = 12 - 8 = \mathbf{4}$.
🔴 BISECTION METHOD & ITERATION COUNT Q26 & Q27 [NOTES]

Repeatedly halves an interval $[a,b]$ with opposite signs $f(a) \cdot f(b) < 0$.

$$N \ge \frac{\ln\left(\frac{b-a}{\epsilon}\right)}{\ln 2} = \frac{\ln(1000)}{\ln 2} \approx 9.96 \implies \mathbf{10\text{ iterations}}$$
Q26 Midpoint Tracking ($f(x)=x^3-x-2$ on $[1,2]$):
• Iter 1: $[1, 2] \implies x_1 = 1.5 \implies f(1.5) = -0.125 < 0$
• Iter 2: $[1.5, 2] \implies x_2 = 1.75 \implies f(1.75) = +1.609 > 0$
• Iter 3: $[1.5, 1.75] \implies x_3 = \mathbf{1.625}$
🔴 NEWTON-RAPHSON METHOD & BREAKDOWN Q39 & Q40 [NOTES]
$$x_{n+1} = x_n - \frac{f(x_n)}{f'(x_n)}$$
⚠️ GEOMETRIC BREAKDOWN CONDITION (Q39): Algorithm fails if $f'(x_n) = 0$ (horizontal tangent line causes division by zero).
Q40 Iteration ($x^6-x-1=0, x_0=1.5$):
$x_1 = 1.3004 \implies x_2 = 1.1814 \implies x_3 \approx \mathbf{1.1395}$.

PAGE 6: DIODE DYNAMICS, CONSTANTS & UNITS

Shockley diode derivatives, dynamic resistance calculations, and standard physical constants

🔴 SHOCKLEY DIODE DERIVATIVE & DYNAMIC RESISTANCE ($r_d$)

Question 20 [NOTES]
Shockley Current Equation:
$$I_D = I_S \left(e^{V_D / V_T} - 1\right)$$

Where $I_S$ is saturation current, $V_D$ is diode voltage, and $V_T$ is thermal voltage ($25\text{ mV}$ at $300\text{ K}$).

Dynamic Conductance & Resistance Formulas:
$$g_m = \frac{dI_D}{dV_D} = \frac{I_S}{V_T} e^{V_D / V_T} \approx \frac{I_D}{V_T}$$ $$r_d = \frac{dV_D}{dI_D} = \frac{1}{g_m} \approx \frac{V_T}{I_D}$$
Worked Diode Calculation (Q20): Given $I_S = 4\text{ pA} = 4 \times 10^{-12}\text{ A}, V_T = 0.025\text{ V}, V_D = 0.65\text{ V}$.
1. $I_D = (4 \times 10^{-12}) e^{0.65/0.025} = (4 \times 10^{-12}) e^{26} = 0.783\text{ A}$
2. Dynamic Resistance: $r_d = \frac{V_T}{I_D} = \frac{0.025}{0.783} \approx \mathbf{0.0319\ \Omega \approx 0.031\ \Omega}$ (Option B).

PAGE 7: DON'T CONFUSE THESE (SIDE-BY-SIDE COMPARISONS)

Side-by-side breakdowns of commonly confused theorems, tests, and numerical formulas

1. MEAN VALUE THEOREM vs. ROLLE'S THEOREM
Parameter Mean Value Theorem Rolle's Theorem
Hypothesis Continuous on $[a,b]$, Diff on $(a,b)$ Continuous on $[a,b]$, Diff on $(a,b)$, $f(a)=f(b)$
Conclusion $f'(c) = \frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}$ $f'(c) = 0$
Geometry Tangent parallel to secant Tangent is horizontal
2. FERMAT'S THEOREM vs. 2ND DERIVATIVE TEST
Parameter Fermat's Theorem Second Derivative Test
Purpose Identifies critical points Classifies min vs max
Statement Local extremum at $c \implies f'(c)=0$ $f'(c)=0$ & $f''(c)>0 \implies$ Min ($f''<0 \implies$ Max)
Trap Critical point does NOT guarantee extremum ($x^3$) If $f''(c)=0$, test fails!
3. INTERMEDIATE VALUE THEOREM vs. BISECTION METHOD
Parameter IVT (Theorem) Bisection Method (Algorithm)
Nature Theoretical existence proof Iterative numerical computation
Condition Continuous on $[a,b]$ Continuous with $f(a) \cdot f(b) < 0$
Failure Discontinuity on interval (Q23) Even multiplicity roots (no sign change)
4. FORWARD DIFFERENCE vs. CENTRAL DIFFERENCE
Parameter Forward Difference Central Difference
Formula $\frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}$ $\frac{f(x+h)-f(x-h)}{2h}$
Error Order $O(h)$ (First-order) $O(h^2)$ (Second-order)
Halving $h \to h/2$ Error reduced to $1/2$ Error reduced to $1/4$ (Q22)

PAGE 8: RAPID TRAPS, DECISION TREES & CHECKLIST

5 critical board exam landmines, algorithm selection decision trees, and final reviewer checklist

5 CRITICAL BOARD EXAM LANDMINES

1. Inverse Derivative Swap

Do NOT compute $\frac{1}{f'(b)}$! Solve $f(a)=b$ first to find $a$, then compute $\frac{1}{f'(a)}$.

2. Parametric 2nd Derivative

$\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} \ne \frac{y''(t)}{x''(t)}$! You MUST divide $\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)$ by $x'(t)$.

3. Curvature Radius Inversion

Curvature $\kappa$ and radius $\rho$ are exact reciprocals: $\rho = 1/\kappa$.

4. Newton-Raphson Breakdown

Geometrically breaks down if $f'(x_n) = 0$ (horizontal tangent line causing division by zero).

5. Open-Top Surface Area

Open top enclosure has ONLY 1 base ($x^2$), not $2x^2$! Objective is $S(x) = x^2 + 4000/x$.

ROOT-FINDING ALGORITHM SELECTOR
Is guaranteed convergence on $[a,b]$ needed? ➔ BISECTION METHOD ($c = \frac{a+b}{2}$, requires $f(a)f(b)<0$).
Is fast quadratic convergence from guess $x_0$ required? ➔ NEWTON-RAPHSON ($x_{n+1} = x_n - \frac{f(x_n)}{f'(x_n)}$, requires $f'(x_n) \ne 0$).
HESSIAN CRITICAL POINT CLASSIFICATION TREE
Calculate Discriminant: $D = f_{xx} f_{yy} - (f_{xy})^2$
• $D > 0$ and $f_{xx} > 0 \implies$ LOCAL MINIMUM
• $D > 0$ and $f_{xx} < 0 \implies$ LOCAL MAXIMUM
• $D < 0 \implies$ SADDLE POINT (No extremum!)
• $D = 0 \implies$ TEST FAILS (Inconclusive)

Q1: Continuity Condition Ans: B

Which condition is necessary for continuity at $x=a$?

A. $f'(a)$ exists | B. $\lim_{x\to a} f(x) = f(a)$ [CORRECT] | C. $\lim_{x\to a} f(x)$ exists | D. Function is differentiable
Solution: Continuity requires 3 conditions: $f(a)$ exists, limit exists, and $\lim_{x\to a} f(x) = f(a)$.
Q2: Discontinuity of Rational Expression Ans: D

Analyze behavior of $f(x) = \frac{x^2-1}{x-1}$ at $x=1$.

A. Point Discontinuity | B. Jump Discontinuity | C. Infinite Discontinuity | D. Removable Discontinuity [CORRECT]
Solution: $f(x) = \frac{(x-1)(x+1)}{x-1} = x+1$ for $x \ne 1$. Common factor cancels $\implies$ Removable hole at $(1,2)$.
Q3: Theoretical Limit $\cos(x)/x$ Ans: D

Evaluate exact limit $\lim_{x\to 0} \frac{\cos(x)}{x}$.

A. 1 | B. 0 | C. -1 | D. Undefined / Does Not Exist [CORRECT]
Solution: As $x \to 0^+$, $\frac{1}{x} \to +\infty$. As $x \to 0^-$, $\frac{1}{x} \to -\infty$. One-sided limits mismatch $\implies$ DNE.
Q4: Multivariable Path Limit Ans: D

Evaluate $\lim_{(x,y)\to (0,0)} \frac{xy}{x^2+y^2}$.

A. 0 | B. 0.5 | C. 1 | D. Does Not Exist [CORRECT]
Solution: Substitute $y = mx \implies \lim = \frac{m}{1+m^2}$. Value depends on path slope $m \implies$ DNE.
Q5: Fundamental Derivative Definition Ans: B

Evaluate $\lim_{h\to 0} \frac{\sin(\pi/6+h) - \sin(\pi/6)}{h}$.

A. 0.5 | B. 0.866 ($\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$) [CORRECT] | C. 0 | D. Does not exist
Solution: Limit equals $\left.\frac{d}{dx}(\sin x)\right|_{x=\pi/6} = \cos(\pi/6) = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \approx 0.866$.
Q6: 100th Derivative of $\cos(x)$ Ans: C

Find 100th derivative of $f(x) = \cos x$ evaluated at $x=\pi$.

A. 1 | B. 0 | C. -1 [CORRECT] | D. Does not exist
Solution: Period is 4. $100 \pmod 4 = 0 \implies f^{(100)}(x) = \cos x \implies \cos(\pi) = -1$.
Q7: Composite Chain Rule Evaluation Ans: B

$h(x) = f(g(x^2))$. Given $g(4)=2, g'(4)=-3, f'(2)=5$. Find $h'(2)$.

A. -30 | B. -60 [CORRECT] | C. 20 | D. -15
Solution: $h'(x) = f'(g(x^2)) \cdot g'(x^2) \cdot 2x \implies h'(2) = f'(g(4)) \cdot g'(4) \cdot 4 = (5)(-3)(4) = -60$.
Q8: Implicit Radical Curve Slope Ans: C

Find slope of tangent to $\sqrt{x} + \sqrt{y} = \sqrt{k}$ at $(x_0, y_0)$.

A. $\sqrt{\frac{y_0}{x_0}}$ | B. $-\frac{y_0}{x_0}$ | C. $-\sqrt{\frac{y_0}{x_0}}$ [CORRECT] | D. $\frac{\sqrt{k}}{\sqrt{x_0}}$
Solution: Differentiate: $\frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}} + \frac{1}{2\sqrt{y}}y' = 0 \implies y' = -\frac{\sqrt{y}}{\sqrt{x}} = -\sqrt{\frac{y_0}{x_0}}$.
Q9: Position-Dependent Acceleration Ans: C

$v(x) = x^2 - 4x\text{ m/s}$. Find $a$ at $x = 5\text{ m}$.

A. $6\text{ m/s}^2$ | B. $5\text{ m/s}^2$ | C. $30\text{ m/s}^2$ [CORRECT] | D. $1\text{ m/s}^2$
Solution: $v(5) = 5$, $\frac{dv}{dx} = 2x-4 \implies \left.\frac{dv}{dx}\right|_{x=5} = 6$. $a = v \frac{dv}{dx} = (5)(6) = 30\text{ m/s}^2$.
Q10: Slowing Down Time Interval Ans: C

$s(t) = t^3 - 6t^2 + 9t$. When is the dampener strictly slowing down?

A. $12$ | C. $0 | D. $t<2$
Solution: Slowing down requires $v(t) \cdot a(t) < 0$. $v = 3(t-1)(t-3)$, $a = 6(t-2)$. Signs differ on $(0,1)$ and $(2,3)$.
Q11: Camera Tracking Angular Velocity Ans: B

$x=2000\text{ m}, dy/dt=500\text{ m/s}, y=2000\text{ m}$. Find $d\theta/dt$.

A. 0.250 rad/s | B. 0.125 rad/s [CORRECT] | C. $45^\circ/s$ | D. 0.500 rad/s
Solution: $\tan\theta = y/2000 \implies \sec^2\theta d\theta/dt = (1/2000) dy/dt \implies 2 d\theta/dt = 0.25 \implies d\theta/dt = 0.125\text{ rad/s}$.
Q12: Exponential Parametric Response Ans: D

$y(t) = e^{2t}, x(t) = e^t$ for $t > 0$. Classify the response curve.

A. Exponential | B. Linear | C. Logarithmic | D. Quadratic [CORRECT]
Solution: $y = (e^t)^2 = x^2 \implies$ Quadratic parabola in Cartesian coordinates.
Q13: Logarithmic Parametric Filter Envelope Ans: B

$x(t) = \ln(t^2), y(t) = \ln(t^5)$ for $t > 1$. What is the mathematical nature of $y$ w.r.t $x$?
